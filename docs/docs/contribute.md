# Contribute to ThaiOML

ThaiOML is a community-maintained, expert-reviewed medical education database. We welcome contributions from medical students, professionals, and domain experts.

## Content Management System (CMS)

We use Decap CMS to manage our articles, abbreviations, and categories. 

[Go to CMS (Login Required)](http://localhost:3000/contribute){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

*Note: You must have an authorized editor account to access the CMS.*

If you do not have permission and would like to join as an editor, please contact us at [code@psira.me](mailto:code@psira.me).

## Editorial Guidelines

Before you start contributing or reviewing, please familiarize yourself with our guidelines:

* [**For Authors**](guidelines/author-guideline.md) - Guidelines for writing and structuring new medical articles.
* [**For Reviewers**](guidelines/reviewer-guideline.md) - Checklists and standards for peer-reviewing submissions.
* [**For Editors**](guidelines/editor-guideline.md) - Workflow instructions for publishing and managing content.
