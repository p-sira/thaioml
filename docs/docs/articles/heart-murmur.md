# Heart Murmur

## Evaluation

- **Murmur Grading (Levine Scale)**
    - **Grade 1**: Audible when carefully listening
    - **Grade 2**: Faint
    - **Grade 3**: Immediately audible
    - **Grade 4**: **Loud with thrills**
    - **Grade 5**: Audible with only the rim of the stethoscope, has thrills
    - **Grade 6**: Audible even when the stethoscope is lifted off the chest

- **Murmur by Location**
    - **RUSB**: **Aortic Stenosis (AS)**, **Aortic Regurgitation (AR)** (root-type), HOCM (less common)
    - **LUSB**: **Pulmonary Stenosis (PS)**, **Pulmonary Regurgitation (PR)**, **AR** (valve-type), **Atrial Septal Defect (ASD)**
    - **Erb's point**: **AR**
    - **LLSB**: **HOCM**, **VSD**, **Tricuspid Stenosis (TS)**, **Tricuspid Regurgitation (TR)**
    - **Apex**: **Mitral Regurgitation (MR)**, **Mitral Valve Prolapse (MVP)**, **Mitral Stenosis (MS)**

- **Specific Etiologies**
    - **Aortic Stenosis**
        - **Systolic ejection murmur** at **RUSB**
        - **Crescendo-decrescendo** pattern (tone, not loudness)
        - > [!IMPORTANT]
          > **High-Yield (NL):** **Radiates to carotid**
        - **Gallavardin phenomenon**: high frequency component filtered and transmitted to the apex (similar to MR)
        - May have **thrills** at RUSB
        - **Pulsus parvus et tardus** at carotid
        - **Sustained heaving** (pressure heaving) at apex: long and heavy impulse of the LV
        - **PMI often not displaced**, unless severe
        - May have **S4 gallop**
    - **Atrial Septal Defect**
        - **Systolic ejection murmur** at **LUSB**
        - Grade < 3 (no thrills)
        - > [!IMPORTANT]
          > **High-Yield (NL):** **Wide fixed split S2**
        - Loud **P2** from pulmonary hypertension (PHT)
    - **Pulmonary Stenosis**
        - **Systolic ejection murmur** at **LUSB**
        - **Single S2**
    - **Mitral Regurgitation**
        - **Holosystolic murmur** at the **apex**
        - **Anterior leaflet** lesion radiates to **axilla**.
        - **Posterior leaflet** lesion radiates to **LLSB**.
    - **Hypertrophic Obstructive Cardiomyopathy**
        - **Systolic ejection murmur** at **LUSB**
