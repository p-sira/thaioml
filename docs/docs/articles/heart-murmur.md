# Heart Murmur

## Murmur Grading

- Also called the Levine scale
- Grade 1: Audible when carefully listening
- Grade 2: Faint
- Grade 3: Immediately audible
- Grade 4: Loud with thrills
- Grade 5: Audible with only the rim of the steth, has thrills
- Grade 6: Audible even when the steth is lifted off the chest


## Murmur by Location

- RUSB: AS, AR (root-type), HOCM (less common)
- LUSB: PS, PR, AR (valve-type), ASD
- Erb's point: AR 
- LLSB: HOCM, VSD, TS, TR
- Apex: MR, MVP, MS

## Aortic Stenosis

- Systolic ejection murmur at RUSB
- Crescendo-decrescendo pattern (tone, not loudness)
- Radiates to carotid
- Gallavardin phenomenon: high frequency component filtered and transmitted to the apex -> similar to MR
- May have thrills at RUSB
- Pulsus parvus et tardus at carotid
- Sustained heaving (pressure heaving) at apex: long and heavy impulse of the LV
- PMI often not displaced, unless severe
- May have S4 gallop

## Atrial Septum Defect

- Systolic ejection murmur at LUSB
- Grade < 3 (no thrills)
- Wide fixed split S2
- Loud P2 from PHT

## Pulmonary Stenosis

- Systolic ejection murmur at LUSB
- Single S2

## Mitral Regurgitation

- Holosystolic murmur at the apex
- Anterior leaflet lesion radiates to axilla.
- Posterior leaflet lesion radiates to LLSB.

## Hypertrophic Obstructive Cardiomyopathy

- Systolic ejection murmur at LUSB
