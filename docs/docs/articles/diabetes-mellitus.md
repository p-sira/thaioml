# Diabetes Mellitus

## Evaluation

- **Diagnostic Criteria**
    - Diagnosis confirmed when **two abnormal results** (or one abnormal result at two different time points) are present:
        - **Fasting Blood Glucose (FBG) ≥ 126 mg/dL**
        - **2h 75g OGTT > 200 mg/dL**
        - **HbA1c ≥ 6.5%**
    - > [!IMPORTANT]
    > **High-Yield (NL):** **Immediate diagnosis** can be made with a **random BG ≥ 200 mg/dL** *plus* classic **hyperglycemic symptoms**.

## Treatment

- **Lifestyle Modification**
    - **Always indicated** as the foundation of management for all patients.

- **Medical Treatment Strategy**
    - **Monotherapy** (Start oral hypoglycemic medication): Indicated if **FBG ≥ 160 mg/dL** or **HbA1c ≥ 8%**
    - **Combination therapy**: Indicated if **FBG ≥ 200 mg/dL** or **HbA1c ≥ 9%**
    - **Insulin therapy addition**: Indicated if **FBG ≥ 300 mg/dL** or **HbA1c ≥ 10%**
