# Diabetes Mellitus

## Diagnosis

- Diagnosis when two abnormal results or one abnormal result at two different time point:
    - FBG >= 126 mg/dL
    - 2h 75g OGTT > 200 mg/dL
    - HbA1c >= 6.5%
- Immediate diagnosis: random BG >= 200 mg/dL with hyperglycemic symptoms

## Management

- Always lifestyle modification

### Medical Treatment

- Start oral hypoglycemic medication: FBG >= 160 mg/dL or HbA1c >= 8%
- Combination therapy: FBG >= 200 mg/dL or HbA1c >= 9%
- Add insulin therapy: FBG >= 300 mg/dL or HbA1c >= 10%
