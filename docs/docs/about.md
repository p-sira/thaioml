# About ThaiOML

**Open Medical Library of Thailand (ThaiOML)** is a community-maintained, expert-reviewed medical education database and AI-powered knowledge base designed for healthcare professionals.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to democratize access to high-quality, up-to-date, and culturally relevant medical information in Thailand. By combining the rigorous standards of peer-reviewed clinical guidelines with modern AI search capabilities, we aim to provide an invaluable tool for clinicians, medical students, and researchers.

## What We Do

1. **Centralized Knowledge Base:** We host a growing collection of clinical trials, treatment protocols, and medical guidelines (e.g., Asthma, Hypertension, Diabetes) organized specifically for the Thai medical context.
2. **AI-Powered Search:** Through our interactive web application, users can query the entire library using natural language, enabling rapid answers to complex clinical questions at the point of care.
3. **Open Collaboration:** ThaiOML relies on a network of dedicated contributors from various medical schools, hospitals, and organizations. Our editorial process ensures that all content is thoroughly vetted before publishing.
