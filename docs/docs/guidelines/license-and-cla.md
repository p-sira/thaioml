# ThaiOML License & Contributor Agreement

ThaiOML is built on the principle of open medical knowledge. To ensure the knowledge remains accessible while protecting the volunteer efforts of our contributors from commercial exploitation, we employ a dual-licensing and Contributor License Agreement (CLA) model.

## 1. Public License: CC-BY-NC 4.0

All content published on ThaiOML is released to the public under the **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC-BY-NC 4.0)** license.

- **You are free to:** Share (copy and redistribute the material) and Adapt (remix, transform, and build upon the material) for non-commercial purposes.
- **Under the following terms:**
  - **Attribution:** You must give appropriate credit to ThaiOML and the original authors, provide a link to the license, and indicate if changes were made.
  - **NonCommercial:** You may not use the material for commercial purposes (e.g., incorporating the content into a paid app, test-prep service, or monetized AI model) without explicit permission from ThaiOML.
  - **No Third-Party AI Ingestion:** Regardless of commercial or non-commercial intent, this content may not be reproduced, copied, scraped, or put into any artificial intelligence program, including large language and generative AI models, by any third party without explicit written permission from ThaiOML. This restriction does not apply to ThaiOML's own internal AI features, such as the "Ask the Library" system, which authors grant permission for via the CLA.

## 2. Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Because ThaiOML is authored by many different contributors, we require a standard CLA to legally manage and protect the project. By submitting content to ThaiOML, you agree to the following terms:

### Copyright Retention
**You retain the copyright to your original work.** Submitting an article to ThaiOML does not strip you of your ownership. You are free to use your own work elsewhere under any terms you wish.

### Grant of Rights
You grant the **ThaiOML Project (and its legal successors or assignees)** a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute your contributions.

### Why do we need this?
This grant of rights empowers ThaiOML to:
1. Publish your work on the platform under the CC-BY-NC 4.0 license.
2. Legally enforce the license against bad actors who scrape or misuse the content commercially.
3. Grant custom commercial licenses (at ThaiOML's discretion) to approved partners that align with our mission to improve medical education. 
4. Seamlessly transition the project to a formal non-profit foundation in the future without needing you to re-sign agreements.
