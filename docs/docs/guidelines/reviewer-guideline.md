# Reviewer Guideline

Reviewers are staff members or medical experts tasked with ensuring the medical accuracy of the content.

* **Priority:** Thai medical knowledge and national clinical guidelines must take precedence over US/international data.
* **Clinical & Exam Accuracy:** In addition to general correctness, explicitly verify the accuracy of the highlighted "High-Yield" facts for the Thai National Licensing Exam (NL).
* **Approval:** Reviewers evaluate pending articles for clinical correctness. You may log into the CMS to approve articles directly.
* **Copyright & Licensing:** Reviewers act in an advisory capacity. By reviewing or approving an article, you acknowledge that you do not gain any copyright ownership over the content.
