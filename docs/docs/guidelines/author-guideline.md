# Author Guideline

Authors (Contributors) are the primary creators of content on ThaiOML.

* **Content Focus:** Articles must cater to medical students. 
* **Writing Style:** You must adopt a high-yield, bulleted format: strictly use a bulleted hierarchy (no long paragraphs), bold key terms heavily, and organize disease articles using standard section headers (Introduction, Pathophysiology, Presentation, Evaluation, Treatment, Complications). Include high-yield facts specifically for the Thai National Licensing Exam (NL). Do not bold generic category labels (e.g., Mechanism, Causes, Symptoms, Presentation, Evaluation).
* **Language:** Write the core content in English. Thai is permitted specifically for mnemonics.
* **Formatting & Visuals:** All content is written in Markdown. Integrate clinical images and `mermaid` flowcharts directly within the relevant bullet points rather than in separate figure sections.
* **Metadata:** Ensure you fill out the required YAML frontmatter fields (like `type`, `specialty`, and `tags`) via the Decap CMS form.
* **Status:** When creating a new article or making an edit, set the `review_status` to `draft` or `pending`.
