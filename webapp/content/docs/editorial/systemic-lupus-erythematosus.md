# Systemic lupus erythematosus

**Systemic lupus erythematosus** (**SLE**), is an autoimmune disease in which the immune system mistakenly attacks healthy tissue in many parts of the body.

## Signs and symptoms

Symptoms vary from person to person, and may come and go. Almost everyone with lupus has joint pain and swelling. Some develop arthritis. Frequently affected joints are the fingers, hands, wrists, and knees. Other common symptoms include:

- [chest pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chest_pain "Chest pain") during [respiration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respiration_\(physiology\) "Respiration (physiology)")
- [joint pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_pain "Joint pain") (stiffness and swelling)
- painless [oral ulcer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_ulcer "Oral ulcer")
- [fatigue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatigue "Fatigue")
- [weight loss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weight_loss "Weight loss")
- headaches
- [fever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fever "Fever") with no other cause
- Skin lesions that appear worse after sun exposure
- general discomfort, uneasiness, or ill feeling ([malaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaise "Malaise"))
- [hair loss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hair_loss "Hair loss")
- sensitivity to sunlight
- a ["butterfly" facial rash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malar_rash "Malar rash"), seen in about half of people with SLE
- swollen lymph nodes
