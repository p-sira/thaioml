# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026

Welcome to the Open Medical Library of Thailand (ThaiOML). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
When you sign in using our authentication service (Clerk), we collect basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture provided by your identity provider (e.g., Google).

### 1.2 Telemetry and Analytics
We use PostHog to collect usage telemetry and analytics. We adhere to a strict **Privacy First** architecture. The data we collect includes:
- Page views and navigation paths
- Interactions with features (e.g., searches, document views)
- General device and browser information

We **do not** collect highly sensitive personal health information (PHI) or track users across third-party sites.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the ThaiOML platform and its content.
- Monitor the health, security, and performance of our systems.
- Understand how users interact with our medical library to guide future architectural and content decisions.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
All telemetry data is processed securely through our PostHog integration. Authentication data is securely managed by Clerk and your respective OAuth providers.

## 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the ThaiOML organization administrators.
