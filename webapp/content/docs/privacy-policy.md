# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026

Welcome to the Open Medical Library of Thailand (ThaiOML). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, sharing, and protection of your data.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
When you sign in using our authentication service (Clerk) or via third-party identity providers such as Google, we collect basic profile information provided by the identity provider. This includes your:
- Name
- Email address
- Profile picture

### 1.2 Telemetry and Analytics
We use PostHog to collect usage telemetry and analytics. We adhere to a strict **Privacy First** architecture. The data we collect includes:
- Page views and navigation paths
- Interactions with features (e.g., searches, document views)
- General device and browser information

We **do not** collect highly sensitive personal health information (PHI) or track users across third-party sites.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the ThaiOML platform and its content.
- Authenticate your identity and provide you with a personalized experience (e.g., saving your preferences and reading history).
- Monitor the health, security, and performance of our systems.
- Understand how users interact with our medical library to guide future architectural and content decisions.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share your information in the following circumstances:
- **Service Providers:** We share information with trusted third-party service providers (such as Clerk for authentication and PostHog for analytics) solely to operate our platform. These providers are strictly bound by data processing agreements and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All telemetry data is processed securely through our PostHog integration. Authentication data is securely managed by Clerk and your respective OAuth providers. We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure communication protocols, to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. 
- **Account Data:** Your account information is retained as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account, you can do so by contacting us. Upon request, we will permanently delete your profile data from our active systems within 30 days.
- **Analytics Data:** Telemetry data is automatically anonymized or deleted after 12 months.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of your data, please contact the ThaiOML organization administrators at info@thaioml.org.
